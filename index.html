Cricket-specific edge cases — deterministic checks

Chase completed

Runs needed = 0: immediate win without consuming any balls.

No legal balls

Runs remain and 0 balls left: innings ended. No impossible extra over.

All out

0 wickets remaining: terminal loss when runs are still needed.

Impossible on sixes alone

Required runs above six per legal ball: display a warning, not an impossible mathematical guarantee.

Wides, no-balls, DLS and Super Overs are deliberately excluded from this simplified legal-ball engine. They are flagged as model extensions, not silently simulated.

Methodology & limitations

In a full local analytics workflow, deliveries can be evaluated against match-state expectations, then summarized using pressure-weighted impact, match consistency and sample-size shrinkage. This site demonstrates the interface and pressure logic with fictional inputs.

Data safeguards

No RR proprietary ball-by-ball data, named player rankings, or private data pack is included in this public demo.

Eligibility safeguard

Any real auction shortlist should check retirement, availability, squad status, competition eligibility and sample sufficiency.

Cricket edge cases

Legal deliveries versus wides/no-balls, runouts, super overs, DLS targets, completed chases, and all-out states require separate treatment.

Model limits

Synthetic rankings are not evidence of actual player quality. The pressure index is illustrative, not predictive or calibrated.
